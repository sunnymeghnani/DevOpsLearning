# Practice sites: containers + kubernetes tracks (deep links)

Checked 2026-11-09 (IST night). Verification was done by fetching each URL (curl/WebFetch, HTTP 200 + page title or content read). "verified" means the page was opened and its title or description matched. "snippet" means the URL came from a search result or link list and was not opened.

Platform facts that apply to every row below:

- **SadServers** (`sadservers.com/scenario/<slug>`): runs in the browser as a throwaway VM. "Access: Email" means a free account (email sign-up). "Public" means no sign-up at all. "Paid/Pro" scenarios are excluded here. No card needed.
- **iximiuz Labs** (`labs.iximiuz.com/challenges/<slug>`): runs in the browser as a throwaway VM or cluster. The free Basic plan needs a one-click sign-up but no card. It allows one playground at a time, 1 h of playtime a day, 2 CPU / 4 GB VMs and internet access to safe domains only (pricing page, verified). Many official Docker challenges by iximiuz are marked "Premium Challenge" (paid) and are **excluded**. The community-authored challenges below (slug ends in a hex id) showed no Premium marker, so they are free.
- **K8s Games** (`k8sgames.com`): a browser simulator with no sign-up. Levels are picked from in-app menus, so there is no deep link per level. The campaign has 20 levels and there are 10 timed challenges (titles read from `js/data/CampaignLevels.js` and `js/modes/ChallengeMode.js`).
- **Kubernetes Goat** (`madhuakula.com/kubernetes-goat/docs/scenarios/...`): free and open source with no sign-up. It runs on **your own** throwaway cluster (kind or k3s in a VM). Do not run it on the homelab kubeadm cluster: it is deliberately vulnerable.
- **Wiz Cloud Security Championship** (`cloudsecuritychampionship.com/challenge/N`): a free CTF. The terminal is embedded in the page and runs on a vendor-hosted lab. Login/Register is shown for the leaderboard, and it is not clear whether an account is needed to play. Hints cost points, not money.
