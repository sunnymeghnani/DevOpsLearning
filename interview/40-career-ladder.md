# 40 — Interview practice: Describe experience honestly under interview pressure

[Interview index](README.md) · [Matching handbook module](../40-career-ladder.md)

Stage: All stages: explain honestly, then deepen the evidence. Suggested time: 10–15 minutes.

Provenance: Original practice scenario. Related incidents and references provide context; no claim that an employer asked this exact question.

## M40-Q01 — Main question

An interviewer asks for a production incident you handled, but you are still learning. How do you answer usefully?

Before answering, state the environment/version you need, your first hypothesis, and one observation that could disprove it.

## M40-Q02 — Interviewer twist

They ask what you personally changed and what you would do differently.

What changes in your diagnosis? Explain the next safe check and what each possible result would mean.

<details>
<summary>Answer checkpoints — open after attempting both questions</summary>

### Expected reasoning

State clearly that your example is a lab or a public case study. Explain the symptom, your hypothesis, evidence, bounded experiment, observed result and limits. Distinguish your actions from the source author's actions.

### Why this order

Credible reasoning and learning evidence are stronger than fabricated production ownership.

### Common trap

Presenting these handbook examples as your employment history or claiming a lab proves production scale.

### Verification and next practice

Give a two-minute account of an actually completed lab, or describe a proposed lab explicitly as unperformed.

### Senior extension

Specify blast radius, who must approve a change, a reversible mitigation, the evidence needed to restore normal operation, and what would make you stop or escalate. If no change is justified, say so.

</details>
